# International Fire Code Chapter 9 Review

## Document Control

- Code: International Fire Code
- Edition: Verify per jurisdiction
- Chapter: Fire Protection and Life Safety Systems
- Local amendments: Verify
- Source: Official ICC Digital Codes or licensed copy

## Required Review

Check:

- Required fire alarm system
- Manual initiation
- Automatic detection
- Sprinkler waterflow alarm
- Valve supervision
- Supervising-station connection
- Emergency voice/alarm communication
- High-rise requirements
- Smoke-control interfaces
- Elevator interfaces
- Special occupancy requirements
- Existing-building provisions
- Acceptance testing
- Record maintenance

## Sprinkler Interfaces

Record:

- Riser designation
- Waterflow switch
- Control-valve supervisory switches
- Fire pump signals
- Tank level or temperature signals
- Air-pressure supervision
- Monitoring destination
- Alarm or supervisory classification

## Engineering Rule

Verify the exact code edition and locally amended section before determining that a system or interface is required.
