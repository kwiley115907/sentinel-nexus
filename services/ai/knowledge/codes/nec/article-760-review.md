# NEC Article 760 — Fire Alarm Wiring Review

## Document Control

- Standard: NFPA 70, National Electrical Code
- Article: 760
- Edition: Verify for each project
- Local amendments: Verify
- Source: Authorized NEC access or licensed copy
- Last reviewed: Not yet reviewed

## Purpose

Use this file as a company-created wiring and installation checklist. It is not a reproduction of the NEC.

## Circuit Classification

Verify:

- Power-limited or non-power-limited fire alarm circuit
- Source equipment listing
- Circuit voltage
- Circuit current
- Required conductor insulation
- Required cable marking
- Permitted wiring method
- Required separation from other circuits

## Cable Identification

Record:

- Cable manufacturer
- Part number
- Listing
- Cable marking
- Conductor count
- Gauge
- Shielding
- Plenum, riser, or general-purpose rating
- Power-limited or non-power-limited suitability
- Environmental suitability

## Routing Review

Check:

- Raceway requirements
- Support and securing
- Protection from physical damage
- Penetrations
- Firestopping
- Separation from power conductors
- Shared raceway restrictions
- Abandoned cable removal
- Accessibility of boxes and terminations
- Wet or damp locations
- Underground routing
- Shaft and riser routing

## Splices and Terminations

Check:

- Listed connectors
- Junction-box accessibility
- Terminal ratings
- Polarity
- Shield termination
- Drain-wire termination
- Grounding method
- Strain relief
- Identification and labeling

## Engineering Rule

Do not approve conductor type or routing until the adopted NEC edition, circuit classification, equipment listing, project specification, and local amendments are verified.
