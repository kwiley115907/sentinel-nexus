# NFPA 72 — 2022 Edition Engineering Notes

## Document Information

- Standard: NFPA 72
- Edition: 2022
- Publisher: National Fire Protection Association
- Adoption status: Verify for each project
- AHJ amendments: Verify for each jurisdiction
- Official source: Authorized NFPA access or licensed copy
- Last reviewed: Not yet reviewed

## Use Restrictions

This file contains company-created engineering notes and checklists. It is not a reproduction of NFPA 72.

## General Project Verification

Before using this edition on a project, verify:

- The 2022 edition is the adopted edition
- Local amendments
- Approved drawings
- Project specifications
- Equipment manufacturer
- Equipment model
- Equipment listing
- Sequence of operations
- AHJ requirements

## Initiating Devices

Review:

- Detector type
- Detector listing
- Ceiling type
- Ceiling height
- Beam configuration
- Air movement
- Environmental conditions
- Manufacturer spacing
- Accessibility for testing
- Addressing and circuit information

## Notification Appliances

Review:

- Public or private mode
- Sleeping-area conditions
- Appliance model
- Candela setting
- Wattage setting
- Synchronization
- Audible coverage
- Visible coverage
- Circuit current
- Voltage drop
- End-of-line voltage

## Emergency Control Functions

Review:

- Elevator recall
- Shunt-trip coordination
- Door release
- Door unlocking
- HVAC shutdown
- Smoke-control sequence
- Fan and damper interfaces
- Fire pump monitoring
- Generator monitoring
- Suppression interfaces

## Engineering Rule

Final design must be verified against the authorized 2022 edition, approved documents, manufacturer instructions, and AHJ requirements.
