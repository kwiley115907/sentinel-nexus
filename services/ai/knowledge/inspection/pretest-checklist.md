# Fire Alarm Pretest Checklist

Before a formal acceptance test, confirm that project-specific requirements have been reviewed.

Typical pretest preparation includes:

- Approved drawing set is available
- Current sequence of operations is available
- Device addresses match documentation
- Device labels are installed
- Circuit labels are installed
- Panel and power-supply directories are current
- Supervisory and trouble conditions are tested
- Notification circuits are tested
- Initiating devices are tested
- Interface functions are tested
- Monitoring communication is tested
- Batteries and charging equipment are reviewed
- Ground-fault detection is reviewed
- Open-circuit and short-circuit behavior is reviewed
- Required synchronization is reviewed
- Required audibility and visibility testing is prepared
- Door, elevator, smoke-control, sprinkler, and other interfaces are coordinated
- Deficiencies are documented
- As-built changes are recorded

The actual test scope must follow the approved project documents, adopted requirements, listed manufacturer instructions, and AHJ direction.
